Lesson 1.4.6 Overview

Social Conventions in Customer Service

Total Duration60 Minutes

Practice professional courtesy, turn-taking, empathy, and cultural awareness in realistic customer interactions. Learn to respond naturally to difficult situations without making assumptions or unsupported promises.

01. Recognize

Identify professional and unprofessional customer-service responses.

02. Adapt

Use polite expressions for empathy, apologies, clarification and turn-taking.

03. Apply

Manage realistic customer conversations and adjust your tone.

Encounter · Professional or Unprofessional?

10 min

Read four short customer interactions. Discuss with a partner: What works, what doesn't, and how could the agent improve? Remember that tone and context matter.

Discussion prompts

Which response acknowledges the customer's concern? Which one sounds dismissive? When might a polite interruption be necessary? How can you avoid assumptions about a customer's background?

Clarify · Customer Service Etiquette Toolkit

15 min

Explore the 12 expression cards. Select a card to flip it and see a realistic example, then practice saying the expression with natural intonation. Use the filter to focus on a communication skill.

Professional judgment

Empathy does not mean promising an outcome. Avoid saying “I guarantee it will be fixed today” unless you can confirm that. Acknowledge the concern, ask relevant questions and explain what you can actually do.

Remember · Retrieve, Choose & Build

10 min

Practice the expressions from Clarify in three ways: match language to its purpose, retrieve useful phrases without seeing the full answer, and build professional responses. Complete the tasks independently, then check your answers and discuss any corrections.

Part A · Match the Communication Goal (3 min)

Choose the expression that best serves each communication goal. Each expression can be used once.

Part B · Complete the Agent's Language (3 min)

Type the missing key phrase from the toolkit. Use the word bank, but pay attention to meaning and grammar.

Word bank: review · slowly · patience · clarify · repeat · understand

Part C · Build a Professional Response (4 min)

Choose the best sequence of communication moves for each customer. Then write one natural sentence you would actually say.

Internalize · Repair the Conversation

10 min

Work in pairs. Read each exchange, identify the communication problem, and rewrite the agent's response. Practice the improved version aloud. Aim for a natural B1 response, not a memorized script.

Fluent Use · Customer Interaction Simulation

15 min

Complete two rounds with a partner. Round 1: Student A is the agent and Student B is the customer. Round 2: switch roles. Each student should read only their own card; hide the card before sharing the screen.

Agent goals

Acknowledge the customer's concern, listen without interrupting, ask a relevant question, clarify the next step without promising an unverified outcome, and close professionally.
